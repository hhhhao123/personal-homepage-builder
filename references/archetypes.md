# Homepage Archetypes

Pick one primary archetype and optionally one secondary archetype.

## Academic Researcher

Best for students, researchers, professors, and lab-oriented profiles.

- Primary sections: hero, research focus, selected publication/work, education, interests, contact.
- Tone: credible, precise, calm.
- Risk: looking like a plain CV. Add one personal memory point or visual metaphor.

## Engineer / Builder

Best for software engineers, indie hackers, AI builders, and technical makers.

- Primary sections: hero, projects, technical focus, selected builds, writing, contact.
- Tone: functional, sharp, systems-minded.
- Risk: generic developer portfolio. Add distinctive interaction or personal operating principle.

## Creator / Blogger

Best for self-media creators, writers, newsletter authors, video creators, and podcasters.

- Primary sections: hero, latest content, social/video platforms, topics, audience promise, featured work, subscribe/contact.
- Tone: expressive, editorial, memorable.
- Risk: too much marketing. Keep the person's voice central.

## Designer / Artist

Best for visual creators, photographers, illustrators, UI designers, and musicians.

- Primary sections: hero, selected work, visual gallery, process, biography, contact.
- Tone: sensory, image-led, crafted.
- Risk: beauty without clarity. Keep navigation and work labels useful.

## Consultant / Freelancer

Best for independent professionals, coaches, advisors, service providers.

- Primary sections: hero, offer, proof, services, process, testimonials, contact.
- Tone: trustworthy, useful, direct.
- Risk: generic agency page. Tie service design to the person's actual story.

## Student / Job Seeker

Best for early-career profiles and internship/job applications.

- Primary sections: hero, skills, projects, education, resume, contact.
- Tone: clear, energetic, credible.
- Risk: overclaiming. Emphasize learning trajectory and real artifacts.

## Founder / Personal Brand

Best for entrepreneurs, public speakers, community leaders, and multi-role people.

- Primary sections: hero, mission, ventures, writing/speaking, proof, contact.
- Tone: confident, narrative, strategic.
- Risk: vague slogans. Anchor every claim in concrete work.

## Hybrid Identity

Use when a person has a strong cross-domain memory point.

Examples:
- Researcher + R&B listener: academic content with night-session music atmosphere.
- Engineer + cyclist: systems thinking with motion/route visual metaphors.
- Blogger + teacher: editorial layout with classroom clarity.

Rule: one domain leads the structure; the other adds atmosphere.

## Life And Social Link Hub

Best for users who mainly need a beautiful `github.io` identity page that gathers their public links and shows personality.

- Primary sections: hero, short bio, featured links, social groups, life/photo notes, contact.
- Tone: lightweight, friendly, personal.
- Risk: becoming a plain link tree. Add a clear visual direction and one story/memory point.
